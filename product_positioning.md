Product positioning 

In terms of Who Kivi is for, ig it applies to anyone who has large workloads and complicated workflows in their professional career (especially for people who need to reexplain themselves a lot , have a lot of grunt work to do, want stuff to be organised etc..) 

The problem which I think it solves compared to normal LLMs is constantly understanding the changing world of the user and applying those changes in their workflow. To put it simply, integrability and user understanding I think would be better in the case of Kivi. 

Currently the major difference is established by the level of integrity and ease of use it has and also its unique feature to model user dialects and style of writing. However I feel it can be extended with semantics and more integrations .The combination of the above 2 can make it really powerful and extend its user base and features and more significantly differentiate it wrt to normal LLMs or AI agents. 

The tools Kivi uses are more easily accessible in my opinion then LLMs and combining that with voice + semantics could truly replace the way people use their computers and handle their workflows
